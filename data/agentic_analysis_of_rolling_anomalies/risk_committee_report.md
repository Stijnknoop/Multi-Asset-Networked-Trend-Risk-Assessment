## Strategic Hedging Framework

Our approach to risk management is dynamic and adaptive, prioritizing capital preservation during periods of extreme market stress while actively seeking opportunities in favorable conditions. "EMERGENCY DE-RISK" is reserved for events exhibiting broad, significant downside across core assets or extreme overall market dislocation. "TACTICAL HEDGE" is employed for specific asset class vulnerabilities or pronounced decoupling events where targeted protection or relative value plays are appropriate. "OPPORTUNISTIC ENTRY" is leveraged during synchronized upward momentum or when strong positive decoupling presents clear alpha generation potential. "MAINTAIN POSITION / HOLD" is the default for low-severity events or mixed signals that do not warrant immediate, aggressive action, allowing for continued monitoring and strategic patience. This framework ensures agility and discipline in navigating complex market environments.
