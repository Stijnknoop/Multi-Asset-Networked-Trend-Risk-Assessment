## Strategic Hedging Framework

Our overarching strategy remains agile, prioritizing capital preservation during periods of extreme systemic stress while actively seeking opportunities presented by market dislocations. Events characterized by extreme overall severity, particularly those involving significant negative movements in core equity indices (US500) or broad synchronized downturns, necessitate immediate and decisive de-risking. Conversely, periods of high severity driven by strong, synchronized positive momentum or isolated, robust rallies in specific asset classes are identified as strategic entry points. Tactical hedges are deployed for significant, but contained, negative shocks in primary risk drivers, especially during structural decoupling events, to protect specific exposures without broad market liquidation. Continuous monitoring of cross-asset correlations and isolation scores is paramount to distinguish between idiosyncratic shocks and systemic threats.
