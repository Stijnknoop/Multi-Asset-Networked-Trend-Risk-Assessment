## Strategic Hedging Framework

The recent event waves highlight a persistent and significant influence of **OIL_CRUDE** as a primary triggering asset, frequently driving structural decoupling events. A recurring pattern of **stagflationary signals** (strong positive oil returns coupled with negative equity/gold) is observed, particularly in higher severity events. Conversely, synchronized positive momentum waves, even those with high severity, present clear **opportunistic entry points**.

Our operational risk verdicts reflect a dynamic approach:
*   **EMERGENCY DE-RISK** is reserved for events exhibiting very high to extreme severity (Worst Isolation Score < -0.08) combined with broad negative market movements or severe stagflationary shocks, demanding immediate and substantial reduction in exposure.
*   **TACTICAL HEDGE** is deployed for moderate to high severity events (Worst Isolation Score between -0.04 and -0.08), or when a primary triggering asset (especially OIL_CRUDE) shows extreme volatility (absolute return > 0.5%) even if overall severity is lower, particularly in decoupling scenarios. This targets specific asset exposures to mitigate risk without a full portfolio overhaul.
*   **OPPORTUNISTIC ENTRY** is favored during synchronized positive momentum waves, especially those with higher severity, or when low-to-moderate severity events show strong positive movements in key assets, signaling potential upside.
*   **MAINTAIN POSITION / HOLD** is the default for low severity events (Worst Isolation Score > -0.03) with minor or mixed asset movements, where the market impact does not warrant immediate intervention.

The prevalence of structural decoupling underscores the need for granular, cross-asset risk monitoring. Our strategy will continue to emphasize targeted hedging for commodity-driven volatility and stagflationary pressures, while actively seeking entry points during robust, synchronized market rallies. Continuous calibration of these thresholds and real-time monitoring of primary trigger assets will be paramount to navigating evolving market conditions.
