## Strategic Hedging Framework

Our operational verdicts are derived from a multi-factor analysis, prioritizing capital preservation during systemic threats and opportunistic positioning during favorable shifts.

1.  **EMERGENCY DE-RISK**: Activated for events exhibiting high or extreme severity scores (typically below -0.08) coupled with significant, synchronized negative momentum across core assets (US500, OIL, GOLD), or severe structural decoupling where the US500 experiences a substantial negative shock (e.g., > -0.15%). This mandates immediate reduction of exposure to vulnerable assets.

2.  **TACTICAL HEDGE**: Employed for moderate-to-high severity events (typically between -0.03 and -0.08) or when specific asset classes experience extreme negative movements (e.g., > -0.50% absolute) even if overall market severity is lower. This strategy focuses on targeted protection against specific downside risks, particularly during structural decoupling events, while maintaining exposure to potentially offsetting or positive assets.

3.  **OPPORTUNISTIC ENTRY**: Initiated during synchronized positive momentum waves across multiple asset classes, regardless of the absolute severity score (which can be high for strong positive deviations). This indicates a broad market rally or sector-wide strength, presenting clear entry points for increasing exposure.

4.  **MAINTAIN POSITION / HOLD**: Applied to low severity events (typically above -0.03) characterized by minor market movements, short durations, or offsetting asset class performance where the net portfolio impact is negligible. This verdict signifies that current market conditions do not warrant immediate intervention, allowing existing strategies to play out.

This framework ensures a disciplined, data-driven approach to navigating market volatility, balancing risk mitigation with growth opportunities.
