## Strategic Hedging Framework

Our operational risk verdicts are designed to be dynamic and responsive, leveraging the aggregated macro shock analysis. "EMERGENCY DE-RISK" is reserved for severe, broad-based negative market movements requiring immediate capital preservation. "TACTICAL HEDGE" is deployed for significant, but often asset-specific, downside risks or broader negative momentum that can be mitigated without full de-risking. "OPPORTUNISTIC ENTRY" capitalizes on strong positive momentum, particularly in decoupling scenarios where a key asset shows exceptional performance. "MAINTAIN POSITION / HOLD" is the default for low-severity events or minor fluctuations that do not warrant active intervention, preserving transaction costs and allowing the portfolio to absorb transient noise. This framework ensures that our risk management is both robust and adaptive, aligning with our long-term investment objectives while navigating short-term volatility.
