## Strategic Hedging Framework

Our operational verdicts are designed to be agile and decisive, reflecting the dynamic nature of market shocks. "EMERGENCY DE-RISK" is reserved for severe, broad-based market downturns or critical structural decouplings impacting core equity holdings. "TACTICAL HEDGE" addresses significant, but contained, downside risks to specific asset classes or moderate synchronized pullbacks. "OPPORTUNISTIC ENTRY" is deployed when market dislocations present clear value or when risk-on sentiment is strongly confirmed across multiple assets. "MAINTAIN POSITION / HOLD" is the default for minor fluctuations, commodity-specific volatility not impacting core equity, or favorable market conditions. This framework ensures disciplined risk management and strategic capital allocation in response to evolving market telemetry.
