## Strategic Hedging Framework

Our strategic hedging framework remains focused on dynamic risk-adjusted positioning. For events categorized as **EMERGENCY DE-RISK**, immediate and aggressive reduction of exposure to vulnerable assets, particularly equities and potentially gold, is paramount. This may involve increasing cash holdings, initiating broad market short positions, or deploying inverse ETFs. **TACTICAL HEDGE** scenarios call for targeted adjustments, such as increasing volatility hedges (e.g., VIX futures), establishing short positions in specific destabilizing assets (e.g., commodity futures), or rebalancing sector exposures. **OPPORTUNISTIC ENTRY** verdicts signal moments to strategically increase exposure to assets showing resilience or experiencing potentially overextended, isolated dips, particularly when broader market sentiment (US500) remains positive. For **MAINTAIN POSITION / HOLD** events, the current portfolio allocation is deemed appropriate, and no immediate action is required, allowing for continued monitoring of market dynamics. Our overarching goal is to preserve capital during systemic shocks while capitalizing on transient dislocations.
