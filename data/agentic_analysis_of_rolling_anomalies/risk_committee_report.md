## Strategic Hedging Framework

Our analysis reveals a recurring pattern of "Structural Decoupling" events, often driven by significant movements in OIL_CRUDE, which frequently diverge from US500 and GOLD performance. This necessitates a dynamic and granular hedging strategy.

1.  **Dynamic Commodity Hedging:** Given the frequent and often extreme movements in OIL_CRUDE, both positive and negative, our commodity exposure requires continuous monitoring and the ability to rapidly deploy long or short hedges. This includes options strategies to capture upside or protect against downside without fully liquidating core positions.
2.  **Equity and Gold Protection:** In decoupling scenarios where US500 or GOLD face significant negative pressure while other assets diverge, tactical hedges (e.g., index futures, put options) are crucial to protect capital.
3.  **Capitalizing on Synchronized Momentum:** For "Synchronized Momentum Waves," particularly positive ones, our strategy is to swiftly identify and capitalize on broad market rallies through increased exposure to high-beta assets or sector-specific ETFs. Negative synchronized waves demand immediate, broad-based de-risking.
4.  **Severity-Based Response Matrix:** The `severity_worst_isolation_score` serves as a critical trigger. Scores indicating high or extreme severity, especially when coupled with negative equity performance, will automatically escalate to an "EMERGENCY DE-RISK" protocol. Moderate severity events, particularly decoupling, will trigger "TACTICAL HEDGE" or "OPPORTUNISTIC ENTRY" based on the directional impact on core holdings. Low severity events will generally result in "MAINTAIN POSITION / HOLD" with enhanced monitoring.

This framework ensures that our responses are calibrated to the specific nature and severity of each market shock, optimizing for both risk mitigation and opportunistic gains.
