## Strategic Hedging Framework

Our approach to risk management remains dynamic, prioritizing capital preservation during periods of synchronized market downturns and strategically deploying capital during opportunistic phases. Structural decoupling events are carefully assessed for their potential to spill over into core equity holdings, with tactical hedges implemented when severity metrics indicate underlying systemic stress, even if primary equity indices appear resilient. We leverage our advanced analytics to distinguish between localized volatility and broader market shifts, ensuring our operational verdicts are precise and timely.
