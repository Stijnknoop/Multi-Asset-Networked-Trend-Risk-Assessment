## Strategic Hedging Framework

Our analysis reveals a recurring pattern of "Structural Decoupling" events, often driven by extreme movements in crude oil, frequently against the backdrop of equity and gold performance. When these decoupling events coincide with high overall market severity and significant downside in core equity holdings (US500), an **EMERGENCY DE-RISK** posture is critical to protect capital.

For less severe decoupling events, or those where core equity positions remain stable or positive despite commodity volatility, a **TACTICAL HEDGE** is recommended. This allows for targeted risk mitigation on specific asset classes (e.g., shorting oil or gold, or hedging equity exposure) without a full portfolio overhaul.

**OPPORTUNISTIC ENTRY** is warranted during clear "Synchronized Momentum Wave (Upward)" events, especially when accompanied by high severity, indicating strong, broad-based positive market conviction. Similarly, minor dips in core assets during otherwise strong commodity-led environments can present entry points.

Finally, **MAINTAIN POSITION / HOLD** is the default stance for low-severity events, minor market noise, or mixed signals where no strong directional conviction or immediate threat/opportunity is present. This preserves transaction costs and avoids over-trading.

The prevalence of crude oil as a primary risk driver, both positively and negatively, highlights its systemic importance and the need for continuous, granular monitoring of commodity markets. Our framework prioritizes swift, decisive action based on aggregated macro shock profiles, ensuring our fund remains resilient and responsive to evolving market dynamics.
