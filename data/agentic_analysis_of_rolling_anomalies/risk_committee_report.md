## Strategic Hedging Framework

Our risk posture is dynamically adjusted based on the aggregated macro shock analysis. "EMERGENCY DE-RISK" mandates immediate, broad-based portfolio protection and reduction of exposure to vulnerable assets, typically triggered by severe, widespread negative movements or critical structural dislocations impacting core holdings. "TACTICAL HEDGE" involves targeted, asset-specific or cross-asset hedging strategies to mitigate identified downside risks in specific market segments, particularly during periods of structural decoupling or moderate synchronized downturns. "OPPORTUNISTIC ENTRY" is reserved for clear, synchronized upward momentum or strong positive divergences, allowing us to capitalize on identified growth vectors. For events of low severity or mixed, transient signals, a "MAINTAIN POSITION / HOLD" stance is adopted, avoiding over-trading and preserving capital for more decisive actions. This framework ensures agility and discipline in navigating complex market volatility.
