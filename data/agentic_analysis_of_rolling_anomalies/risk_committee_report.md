## Strategic Hedging Framework

Our analysis reveals a recurring pattern of structural decoupling, often triggered by significant movements in OIL_CRUDE, with varying impacts on US500 and GOLD. Synchronized momentum waves, both positive and negative, also present clear opportunities or risks.

*   **Emergency De-Risk:** Reserved for events exhibiting extremely high severity (Worst Isolation Score below -0.08 to -0.10, or lower) coupled with significant negative movements across multiple core assets, especially US500. These events signal systemic stress requiring immediate capital preservation.
*   **Tactical Hedge:** Applied to high or moderate-high severity events, particularly those with structural decoupling where US500 shows negative returns, or where a key asset (e.g., OIL_CRUDE, GOLD) experiences a substantial adverse move that could impact specific portfolio segments. This allows for targeted protection without full market exit.
*   **Opportunistic Entry:** Utilized for events characterized by synchronized positive momentum across assets, or structural decoupling where US500 and other key assets show strong positive returns, even if one asset is negative. This indicates a favorable environment for increasing exposure.
*   **Maintain Position / Hold:** Employed for low to very low severity events, or those with mixed signals where the overall market impact is contained and does not warrant immediate action. This reflects confidence in existing portfolio construction to weather minor fluctuations.

The prevalence of "Structural Decoupling" events, often with OIL_CRUDE as the primary trigger, underscores the importance of dynamic cross-asset correlation monitoring. Our framework prioritizes capital preservation during severe negative synchronized shocks and significant equity downturns, while actively seeking entry points during broad positive momentum or when specific asset strength outweighs contained weaknesses. Continuous monitoring of the "Worst Isolation Score" and the directional bias of US500 remains paramount for timely and effective decision-making.
