## Strategic Hedging Framework

Our analysis reveals a recurring pattern of "Structural Decoupling" events, often triggered by significant movements in OIL_CRUDE, frequently against the direction of US500 and GOLD. "Synchronized Momentum Waves" also occur, presenting either broad market rallies or downturns.

Our operational verdicts are strategically aligned:
*   **EMERGENCY DE-RISK** is reserved for high-severity events (Isolation Score typically below -0.08 to -0.10) where significant negative movements across multiple core assets, or extreme single-asset dislocations, pose a systemic threat to portfolio capital. This mandates immediate reduction of exposure to vulnerable assets.
*   **TACTICAL HEDGE** is deployed for moderate to high-severity events (Isolation Score typically between -0.04 and -0.08), or when a primary triggering asset exhibits substantial movement, particularly during decoupling. This allows for targeted protection of specific exposures or exploitation of relative value opportunities without a full portfolio overhaul.
*   **OPPORTUNISTIC ENTRY** is leveraged during low to moderate-severity events (Isolation Score closer to zero) characterized by synchronized positive momentum or strong positive moves in a primary triggering asset. This signals favorable conditions for increasing exposure.
*   **MAINTAIN POSITION / HOLD** is the default for low-severity events (Isolation Score typically above -0.04) with minor or mixed asset movements, indicating that current market conditions do not warrant immediate, aggressive action and existing positions are deemed appropriate.

This framework ensures a disciplined, risk-adjusted approach to market volatility, prioritizing capital preservation during adverse conditions while actively seeking to capture upside potential. Continuous monitoring of these macro shock waves and their underlying drivers remains paramount for agile risk management.
