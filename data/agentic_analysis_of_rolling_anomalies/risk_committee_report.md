## Strategic Hedging Framework

The observed market behavior indicates a persistent environment of structural decoupling, frequently driven by significant volatility in OIL_CRUDE. While synchronized momentum waves (both positive and negative) occur, the dominant pattern involves divergent asset class performance.

Our strategic framework emphasizes:

1.  **Dynamic Commodity Risk Management:** The high frequency and magnitude of OIL_CRUDE as the primary destabilizing asset necessitate continuous, granular monitoring and a proactive hedging strategy for commodity exposures. Extreme movements in oil, even when other assets are positive, can trigger an **EMERGENCY DE-RISK** for that specific asset class.
2.  **Targeted Equity and Safe-Haven Protection:** Events showing significant negative returns in US500 and/or GOLD, even if partially offset by other assets, warrant **TACTICAL HEDGES**. This protects core portfolio stability against broader market downturns or flight-to-safety dynamics.
3.  **Opportunistic Capital Deployment:** Periods of synchronized positive momentum, particularly those with higher severity scores, present clear **OPPORTUNISTIC ENTRY** points to enhance returns. Similarly, strong positive decoupling in specific assets can be leveraged.
4.  **Prudent Stance on Minor Shocks:** Low severity, short-duration events, especially those with balanced or minimal overall portfolio impact, are best addressed by **MAINTAINING POSITION / HOLD**. Over-reacting to minor noise can lead to unnecessary transaction costs and whipsaw effects.
5.  **Severity and Duration as Key Triggers:** The "Worst Isolation Score" and event duration are critical indicators for escalating risk responses. Scores below -0.10, especially when coupled with significant negative returns in core assets, are direct triggers for **EMERGENCY DE-RISK**. Prolonged events, even at moderate severity, increase the likelihood of a **TACTICAL HEDGE**.

This framework ensures that our risk posture remains adaptive, allowing us to navigate complex market conditions with precision, protecting capital where necessary, and seizing growth opportunities as they emerge.
