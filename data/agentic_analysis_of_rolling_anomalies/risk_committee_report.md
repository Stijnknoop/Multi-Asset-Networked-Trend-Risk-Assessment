## Strategic Hedging Framework

Our institutional risk management philosophy dictates that active hedging or de-risking actions are reserved for events that demonstrate clear systemic implications or significant, correlated deviations across multiple asset classes. The observed telemetry, characterized by fractional deviations and minor intraday fluctuations, falls well within the expected operational noise of the market.

*   **EMERGENCY DE-RISK:** Triggered only by severe, multi-asset, high-magnitude, and highly correlated negative movements indicative of a systemic market dislocation (e.g., >1% simultaneous decline across major equity indices, commodities, and fixed income in a single minute, or a confirmed liquidity crisis).
*   **TACTICAL HEDGE:** Initiated for significant, but not systemic, adverse movements in a primary asset or a moderate, correlated negative shift across a subset of assets (e.g., >0.5% decline in a major index, or >0.2% correlated decline in two key assets, warranting temporary exposure reduction).
*   **OPPORTUNISTIC ENTRY:** Considered for significant, but not systemic, positive movements in a primary asset or a moderate, correlated positive shift across a subset of assets, aligned with pre-defined investment theses (e.g., >0.5% gain in a major index, or >0.2% correlated gain in two key assets, presenting a favorable entry point).
*   **MAINTAIN POSITION / HOLD:** The default stance for all market activity that falls within expected volatility ranges, exhibits uncorrelated movements, or presents magnitudes below the thresholds for tactical intervention. This includes the minor fractional deviations observed in the current telemetry.

The current market environment, as indicated by the telemetry, does not present any signals that necessitate a deviation from our MAINTAIN POSITION / HOLD strategy. We continue to monitor for genuine correlation breaks or significant multi-asset joint moves that would trigger higher-level risk responses.
