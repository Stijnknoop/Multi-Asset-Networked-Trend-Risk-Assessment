## Strategic Hedging Framework

Our strategic hedging framework is designed for dynamic adaptation to both synchronized market movements and structural decoupling events. For periods of high systemic risk, particularly those characterized by significant negative returns in core equity and precious metals alongside extreme volatility in commodities (e.g., crude oil), we prioritize immediate de-risking to protect capital. Tactical hedges are deployed when moderate to high severity events indicate potential short-term downside or significant cross-asset dislocations. Conversely, periods of strong positive momentum or decoupling where core assets perform well against a volatile commodity backdrop are identified as opportunistic entry points to enhance long-term portfolio value. Low-severity events or those with mixed, non-extreme signals warrant maintaining current positions, allowing for market absorption without reactive adjustments. Continuous monitoring of cross-asset correlations and primary risk drivers remains paramount to our risk management mandate.
