## Strategic Hedging Framework

The recent event waves highlight a recurring theme of significant volatility, often driven by commodity markets (particularly OIL_CRUDE), which frequently decouple from broader equity and safe-haven assets. While many events are low to moderate in severity, the presence of several "Very High" and "Extremely High" severity events, especially those involving synchronized negative movements in US500 and Gold, necessitates agile risk management.

Our framework prioritizes:
1.  **Proactive De-risking**: Immediate reduction of exposure during broad, high-severity downturns impacting core equity holdings (e.g., US500).
2.  **Targeted Hedging**: Implementing tactical hedges for specific asset classes (e.g., commodity futures, gold options) during structural decoupling events where a primary risk driver exhibits extreme volatility, even if the broader market is stable.
3.  **Opportunistic Capital Deployment**: Identifying and acting on synchronized positive momentum waves, particularly those with high severity, as clear entry points for strategic allocation.
4.  **Maintaining Core Positions**: Exercising discretion for low-severity events or those with contained, non-systemic impacts, avoiding over-trading.

Continuous monitoring of cross-asset correlations and the "Worst Isolation Score" will remain critical to distinguish between transient noise and systemic threats, ensuring our operational responses are both timely and proportionate.
