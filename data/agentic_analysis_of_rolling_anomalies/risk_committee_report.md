## Strategic Hedging Framework

Our analysis reveals a recurring pattern of "Structural Decoupling" events, often driven by significant shocks in the OIL_CRUDE market. These events frequently lead to inverse reactions in equities (US500) and gold (GOLD), presenting both substantial risks and clear opportunities.

**Key Observations:**
*   **Oil as a Primary Driver:** OIL_CRUDE is consistently identified as the primary risk driver, initiating the largest absolute return in a majority of events, whether positive or negative.
*   **Inverse Correlation in Decoupling:** Extreme positive shocks in OIL_CRUDE often correlate with strong negative movements in US500 and GOLD, necessitating immediate de-risking of equity/gold exposure. Conversely, extreme negative shocks in OIL_CRUDE often correlate with strong positive movements in US500 and GOLD, creating significant opportunistic entry points.
*   **Severity Thresholds:** Events with "Worst Isolation Score" exceeding -0.08 (absolute value) often trigger "EMERGENCY DE-RISK" or "OPPORTUNISTIC ENTRY" verdicts, depending on the direction of the decoupling.
