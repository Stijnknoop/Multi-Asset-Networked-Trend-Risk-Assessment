## Strategic Hedging Framework

Our overarching strategy emphasizes dynamic risk management, prioritizing capital preservation during periods of extreme systemic stress. For synchronized market downturns or severe decoupling events where core equity and safe-haven assets experience significant negative returns, immediate de-risking is paramount. Tactical hedges are deployed for more contained, asset-specific volatility or when extreme moves in a single asset (e.g., commodities) present contagion risk. Opportunistic entries are reserved for broad-based, synchronized rallies, while low-impact events are monitored without immediate portfolio adjustments. Continuous monitoring of cross-asset correlations and primary risk drivers remains critical to adapt our posture swiftly and effectively.
