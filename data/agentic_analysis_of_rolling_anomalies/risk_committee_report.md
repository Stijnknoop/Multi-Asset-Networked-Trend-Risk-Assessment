## Strategic Hedging Framework

Our risk management framework is designed to dynamically adapt to evolving market conditions. "EMERGENCY DE-RISK" is reserved for extreme, systemic threats requiring immediate and substantial reduction in exposure. "TACTICAL HEDGE" is deployed for significant, but non-systemic, downside risks or heightened uncertainty, aiming for targeted, temporary protection. "OPPORTUNISTIC ENTRY" capitalizes on temporary market dislocations where underlying fundamentals remain sound and a rebound is anticipated. "MAINTAIN POSITION / HOLD" signifies that current market movements are within expected volatility or align with our strategic outlook, requiring no immediate portfolio adjustments. This adaptive approach ensures capital preservation while remaining agile to capture alpha opportunities.
