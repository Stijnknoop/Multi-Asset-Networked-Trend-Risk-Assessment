## Strategic Hedging Framework

Our operational risk verdicts are designed to be agile and responsive to the evolving macro landscape. We prioritize capital preservation during synchronized downturns (EMERGENCY DE-RISK), implement targeted protection for specific exposures or potential reversals (TACTICAL HEDGE), and strategically deploy capital during periods of confirmed positive momentum or attractive valuations (OPPORTUNISTIC ENTRY). For events that do not pose a material threat to our core portfolio or exhibit contained, low-severity movements, we advocate for a disciplined MAINTAIN POSITION / HOLD stance. This framework ensures that our risk management actions are proportionate to the observed market behavior and aligned with our long-term investment objectives.
