## Strategic Hedging Framework

Our overarching strategy remains focused on dynamic risk management, leveraging our advanced analytics to identify and categorize macro shock events. We prioritize capital preservation during periods of systemic stress (EMERGENCY DE-RISK), implement targeted adjustments for evolving market regimes (TACTICAL HEDGE), and capitalize on favorable conditions (OPPORTUNISTIC ENTRY). For events presenting mixed signals or contained impacts, we maintain a disciplined posture (MAINTAIN POSITION / HOLD), avoiding overreaction. Continuous monitoring of cross-asset correlations and primary risk drivers is paramount to inform these decisive operational verdicts and safeguard portfolio integrity.
