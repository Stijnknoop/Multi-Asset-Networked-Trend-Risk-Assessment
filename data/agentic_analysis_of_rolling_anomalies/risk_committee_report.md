## Strategic Hedging Framework

The recent event log highlights a recurring pattern of "Structural Decoupling" events, frequently driven by significant volatility in OIL_CRUDE. While many of these events are contained, those with "Very High" or "Extreme" severity, especially when coupled with negative US500 performance, necessitate immediate de-risking. Conversely, strong positive synchronized momentum waves, even with high severity scores (which in this context reflect magnitude of positive movement), present clear opportunistic entry points.

Our operational risk verdicts are calibrated to respond dynamically:
*   **EMERGENCY DE-RISK:** Reserved for events demonstrating severe, broad-based market stress or critical equity depreciation, often indicated by a Worst Isolation Score below -0.08 and negative US500 returns, or synchronized negative momentum. This mandates immediate reduction of risk exposure.
*   **TACTICAL HEDGE:** Applied to moderate-to-high severity events, particularly structural decouplings where a primary asset (often OIL_CRUDE) exhibits significant movement, or when US500 shows negative but not catastrophic returns. This allows for targeted protection of specific exposures without a full portfolio overhaul.
*   **OPPORTUNISTIC ENTRY:** Utilized during strong, synchronized positive momentum waves or structural decouplings where US500 demonstrates robust positive performance despite volatility in other assets. This signals favorable conditions for increasing exposure.
*   **MAINTAIN POSITION / HOLD:** Employed for low-severity events, short-duration noise, or mixed signals that do not present a clear directional imperative for immediate action, indicating current portfolio settings are adequate.

This framework ensures that our responses are proportionate to the observed market dynamics, allowing us to navigate periods of heightened volatility with precision and strategic intent. Continuous monitoring of these macro shock waves remains paramount for agile risk management.
