## Strategic Hedging Framework

The recent event waves highlight a recurring pattern of **Structural Decoupling**, often driven by significant movements in OIL_CRUDE. While many of these events are low to moderate in severity and do not warrant immediate portfolio-wide de-risking, the instances of **Extremely High Severity** (e.g., Event IDs 35, 41, 55, 81, 93) demand immediate and decisive action. These events are characterized by severe negative impacts on US500 and GOLD, even when OIL_CRUDE shows strong positive returns, indicating a broad flight from risk and traditional safe-havens.

Our framework prioritizes:
1.  **Rapid De-risking:** For extreme, broad-based negative shocks or severe decoupling where core equity and safe-haven assets are significantly impaired.
2.  **Targeted Hedging:** For high-severity events or those with notable downside in specific asset classes (e.g., US500, GOLD) even if overall market dynamics are mixed. This protects against specific exposures without overreacting to contained volatility.
