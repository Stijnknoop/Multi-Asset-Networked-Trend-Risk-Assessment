## Strategic Hedging Framework

The analysis reveals a recurring pattern of "Structural Decoupling" events, often driven by significant movements in OIL_CRUDE. When OIL_CRUDE rallies strongly (e.g., Events 5, 7, 13, 34, 35, 41, 43, 44, 47, 49, 54, 55, 69, 71, 73, 75, 78, 81, 88, 90, 92, 93, 96, 99, 100, 104, 105, 109, 110, 111, 114), it frequently coincides with negative or flat performance in US500 and GOLD, indicating inflationary pressures or supply shocks that are detrimental to broader equity markets. Conversely, significant declines in OIL_CRUDE (e.g., Events 9, 10, 11, 15, 16, 27, 28, 36, 40, 42, 45, 46, 50, 51, 52, 53, 56, 57, 58, 66, 68, 70, 72, 74, 76, 77, 79, 85, 86, 87, 91, 95, 97, 98, 102, 106, 107, 112, 115, 118) often present "Opportunistic Entry" points for equities and gold, as reduced energy costs typically boost economic sentiment and corporate earnings.

"Synchronized Momentum" events, whether positive or negative, tend to be more straightforward. Broad positive rallies (e.g., Events 2, 19, 24, 25, 26, 30, 48, 60, 63, 64, 65, 82, 84, 94, 103, 113, 117, 119) are generally "Opportunistic Entry" signals, while broad negative movements (e.g., Events 3, 18, 22, 23, 31, 59, 61, 101) warrant "Tactical Hedge" or "Emergency De-Risk" actions depending on severity.

The most severe events (Isolation Score < -0.10, particularly < -0.15) consistently involve extreme movements in OIL_CRUDE, often leading to "EMERGENCY DE-RISK" verdicts.
