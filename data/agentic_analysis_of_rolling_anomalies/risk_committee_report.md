## Strategic Hedging Framework

Our analysis of these volatility waves underscores the critical importance of dynamic risk management. The prevalence of "Structural Decoupling" events, particularly those triggered by extreme movements in OIL_CRUDE, highlights the need for robust cross-asset correlation monitoring and scenario planning. "Synchronized Momentum Waves" provide clear signals for either broad market de-risking or opportunistic entry. The identified "EMERGENCY DE-RISK" events (IDs 35, 41, 44, 55) represent critical junctures where systemic risk was elevated, demanding immediate and significant portfolio adjustments to preserve capital. "TACTICAL HEDGE" verdicts are issued for high-severity, directional risks that can be mitigated without full portfolio liquidation. "OPPORTUNISTIC ENTRY" signals capitalize on clear positive momentum or specific asset dips. Finally, "MAINTAIN POSITION / HOLD" verdicts are reserved for events with contained severity or balanced cross-asset movements, indicating that current exposures remain within acceptable risk parameters. This framework ensures agile responses to evolving market conditions, optimizing risk-adjusted returns.
