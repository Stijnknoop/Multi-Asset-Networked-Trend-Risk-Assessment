*   **Systemic Risk vs. Relative Value:** We must distinguish between events signaling broad systemic risk (e.g., synchronized negative waves, or extreme crashes in both equities and gold) requiring **EMERGENCY DE-RISK**, and those presenting relative value plays (e.g., one asset class significantly outperforming/underperforming others) warranting **TACTICAL HEDGE**.
*   **Equity and Gold as Bellwethers:** Significant declines in US500 and/or GOLD, especially when coupled, are strong indicators for broader de-risking, even if other assets show strength.
*   **Opportunistic Entries:** Synchronized positive momentum waves, particularly those with high velocity, offer clear windows for **OPPORTUNISTIC ENTRY**.
