## Strategic Hedging Framework

Our approach to managing market volatility is rooted in a dynamic, event-driven framework. For "Synchronized Momentum (all negative)" events, particularly those with high severity and significant asset declines, an **EMERGENCY DE-RISK** posture is adopted to aggressively reduce exposure and preserve capital. Conversely, "Synchronized Momentum (all positive)" events, especially those with strong upward trends, signal **OPPORTUNISTIC ENTRY** to capitalize on broad market rallies.

"Structural Decoupling (mixed directions)" events, characterized by divergent asset performance and varying severity, typically warrant a **TACTICAL HEDGE**. This allows us to selectively protect against downside risks in specific assets while maintaining exposure to those showing resilience or positive momentum. For minor, low-severity events with no clear directional bias or significant impact, a **MAINTAIN POSITION / HOLD** strategy is preferred, avoiding unnecessary transaction costs and market noise.

This framework ensures that our responses are calibrated to the specific nature and severity of each macro shock, optimizing risk-adjusted returns and safeguarding the fund's capital. Continuous monitoring and rapid assessment remain paramount to our operational agility.
