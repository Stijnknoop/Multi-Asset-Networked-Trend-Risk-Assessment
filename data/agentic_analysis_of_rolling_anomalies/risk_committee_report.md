Our tactical hedging strategy in response to these signals is designed to:
1.  **Mitigate Specific Asset Volatility**: Address pronounced, short-term directional moves in individual commodity exposures (e.g., rapid drops in Crude Oil or Gold, or sharp spikes in Crude Oil).
2.  **Manage Cross-Asset Divergence**: Adjust for temporary breakdowns or shifts in typical inter-commodity correlations, particularly between Crude Oil and Gold, which have shown repeated inverse movements.
3.  **Preserve Capital**: Implement targeted hedges to protect against potential continuation of adverse movements or to lock in gains from sharp spikes, without disrupting broader portfolio allocations.

The absence of widespread, correlated adverse movements across all major asset classes (US500, Crude Oil, Gold) suggests that the observed volatility is primarily idiosyncratic to the commodity complex or specific risk-off/risk-on rotations within that segment, rather than a broad market deleveraging event. Therefore, a surgical, tactical hedging approach remains appropriate, focusing on the specific assets exhibiting significant deviations. We will continue to monitor for any escalation in correlation breaks or magnitude of movements that could necessitate a higher-tier risk response.
