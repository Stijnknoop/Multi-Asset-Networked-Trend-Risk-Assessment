## Strategic Hedging Framework

The recent volatility waves highlight a persistent influence of commodity markets, particularly OIL_CRUDE, as a primary destabilization trigger. Structural decoupling events are frequent, demanding granular risk management rather than broad-brush responses.

Our strategic framework will prioritize:

1.  **Dynamic Commodity Risk Management:** Given the recurring and often extreme movements in OIL_CRUDE, especially during structural decoupling events, we must maintain agile hedging strategies for our commodity exposures. This includes options, futures, and other derivatives to mitigate downside risk or capture upside where appropriate, even when broader equity markets remain stable or positive.
2.  **Equity Downside Protection:** For high-severity events where the US500 experiences significant negative returns, immediate de-risking protocols are paramount. This involves pre-defined triggers for reducing equity exposure or implementing portfolio-level hedges (e.g., index futures, VIX products) to protect capital.
3.  **Opportunistic Capital Deployment:** Synchronized momentum waves, particularly those exhibiting strong positive returns across assets, represent clear opportunities for capital deployment. Our systems should be primed to identify these "risk-on" signals, even if accompanied by high volatility (as measured by isolation scores), to enhance portfolio returns.
4.  **Measured Response to Low Severity:** For events characterized by very low severity or mixed asset movements without significant impact on core equity holdings, a "MAINTAIN POSITION / HOLD" stance is appropriate. Over-trading on minor fluctuations can erode alpha and increase transaction costs. Our focus remains on macro-level shocks that genuinely threaten or enhance portfolio objectives.

This framework ensures that our operational risk verdicts are systematic, responsive, and aligned with our overarching investment objectives, balancing capital preservation with opportunistic growth.
