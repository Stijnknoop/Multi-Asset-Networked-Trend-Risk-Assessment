## Strategic Hedging Framework

Our risk posture remains dynamic, calibrated to macro shock characteristics. For events exhibiting extreme systemic risk (Severity < -0.10, especially with multi-asset downside or unprecedented single-asset volatility), immediate **EMERGENCY DE-RISK** protocols are activated to preserve capital. Significant, but contained, structural decouplings or moderate synchronized negative momentum trigger **TACTICAL HEDGE** strategies, focusing on targeted protection for specific exposures or relative value plays. Conversely, high-severity synchronized positive momentum waves are identified as **OPPORTUNISTIC ENTRY** points, allowing us to strategically increase exposure and capture alpha. All other events, falling within acceptable volatility thresholds or presenting no clear actionable signal, warrant a **MAINTAIN POSITION / HOLD** stance, ensuring we avoid over-trading minor fluctuations. This framework prioritizes capital preservation during severe downturns, agile protection during specific market dislocations, and aggressive positioning during confirmed rallies.
